# Users sign in with a password that unlocks the store, and form one local Team

Supersedes the "no app lock" part of ADR 0004; encryption at rest and the audit log stay as ADR 0004 decided.

Several people now use the same app on the same machine, as one Team: a Team Leader invites Members by email, every Member lists their Skills and links their own Jira and Trello users, and the owner of a Proposed Action is suggested from those Skills. Everything stays on this machine: no server, no sync between machines, no external service (ADR 0001).

The app opens on a sign-in screen. Each User has an email, a name, a password and a list of Skills; one User is signed in at a time and is the one recorded as `approved_by` in the audit log. The password unlocks the store: the store keeps one random data key, and each User has a key slot, that key encrypted (AES-GCM) with a key derived from their password (scrypt). Without a correct password the store does not open. The first User to sign up creates the store, or wraps the key of a store made before this ADR, and becomes the Team Leader. A new Member cannot open the store alone, so the Team Leader adds their email and the app shows a one-time Invitation code, valid for 7 days, kept as a slot encrypted under that code; the Member signs up with the email and the code, sets a password and joins the Team, and the Invitation slot is replaced by theirs. Only the Team Leader invites and removes Members and connects Jira and Trello, and every send goes through that connection. Every Member edits their own Skills and links their own Jira and Trello users by signing in there once through the same OAuth (Jira, scope `read:jira-user`) and token (Trello, read-only) flows: the app keeps only the user id the service names, never typed, and drops the Member's token (Trello's is revoked). Sessions belong to the Team: every signed-in User sees and decides all of them.

For Corporate and Others, after the Domain stage the local model is given each Proposed Action and the Members' names and Skills, and names the Member best suited, or nobody when no Skill fits, in which case the owner stays empty. It is a suggestion: the Team Leader can pick another Member from the Team before approval, and the Member's Jira or Trello user is sent as the assignee inside the payload the User sees and approves (ADR 0002). Medical stays as it was.

Consequences: a forgotten password cannot be recovered by the app; the Team Leader can invite the Member again, and if the Team Leader is the only User and forgets theirs, only the encrypted backup can bring the data back. Users share one data key by design (Sessions belong to the Team), so signing in protects against other people on the same OS login, not one Member from another. Removing a Member deletes their key slot. Several Teams per store, sync between machines and remote access stay out of scope.
